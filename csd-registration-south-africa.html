Why CSD Registration Matters for South African Suppliers

Learn what Central Supplier Database registration is and how businesses can prepare for South African public-sector opportunities.

Ntuthuko Biz Connect · Updated 8 June 2026

The Central Supplier Database, commonly called the CSD, is a central source of supplier information used by organs of state. Registration is a foundation for businesses pursuing public-sector opportunities.

What CSD registration does

A supplier profile brings core business, ownership, banking and tax information together. Departments and public entities can use this information during procurement.

CSD does not guarantee a tender

Registration makes a business administratively ready, but it does not replace tender research, pricing, capability, experience or the documents requested in each bid.

Keep the profile accurate

Changes to directors, bank accounts, contact information or tax status may affect how a business appears when procurement officials review its profile.

Important: Requirements can change and depend on your circumstances. Confirm current requirements with the relevant authority or a qualified professional.
