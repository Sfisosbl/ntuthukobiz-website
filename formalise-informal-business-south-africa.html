How to Formalise an Informal Business in South Africa

An overview of formalising an informal South African business through registration, tax, banking, compliance and digital credibility.

Ntuthuko Biz Connect · Updated 8 June 2026

Formalising does not erase the resourcefulness that built an informal business. It adds structure that can support contracts, banking, funding, larger customers and sustainable growth.

Choose an appropriate structure

Consider the owners, risks, goals and administrative responsibilities before registering. A private company is common, but the right structure depends on the activity and people involved.

Separate and organise the business

Use clear financial records, suitable banking arrangements, written quotations and invoices, and a basic system for customer and supplier information.

Build a credible public presence

A professional website, Google Business Profile and domain-based email help customers verify and contact the business. Keep public details consistent across every channel.

Important: Requirements can change and depend on your circumstances. Confirm current requirements with the relevant authority or a qualified professional.
