What Is Tax Clearance? A SARS Guide for Small Businesses

Understand SARS tax compliance status, tax clearance PINs and why South African businesses need accurate tax records.

Ntuthuko Biz Connect · Updated 8 June 2026

When customers, funders or tender processes ask for tax clearance, they usually want evidence that a business is compliant with the South African Revenue Service.

Tax compliance status in plain language

A compliant status generally means required returns and payments are up to date, or that acceptable arrangements are in place. Businesses can authorise third parties to verify status through a PIN.

Why businesses lose compliant status

Outstanding returns, unpaid amounts, incorrect registrations or unresolved historic issues can affect the status. First identify the exact issue rather than submitting repeated applications.

Build tax administration into the business

Keep registration details current, store submissions, monitor deadlines and separate business records from personal records. Consistent administration prevents last-minute problems.

Important: Requirements can change and depend on your circumstances. Confirm current requirements with the relevant authority or a qualified professional.
