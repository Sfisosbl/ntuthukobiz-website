02

Company registration

How much does company registration cost?
Our standard company registration service starts from R499.
What documents do I need to register a company?
For a standard registration, we generally require a certified copy of your ID or passport, a contact number, email address and physical address. Certain industries may require additional documentation.
How long does company registration take?
Most registrations are completed within a few business days, depending on CIPC processing times and whether all required information has been submitted correctly.
Can I register a company if I am employed?
Yes. Employment does not generally prevent you from owning or registering a business, but you should check whether your employment contract restricts outside business activities or creates a conflict of interest.
03

Tax and compliance

Can you help with SARS registrations?
Yes. We assist with tax number, VAT, PAYE and UIF registrations, tax compliance support and SARS profile assistance.
What is a Tax Compliance Status or tax clearance?
A Tax Compliance Status indicates whether your tax affairs are in good standing with SARS. Many tenders, funding opportunities and corporate clients request proof of this status.
What is CSD registration?
The Central Supplier Database is a government supplier database used by public institutions when procuring goods and services. Registration is often required before doing business with government entities.
Do I need compliance if my business is small?
Yes. The exact obligations depend on the business, but good compliance protects operations, improves credibility and helps position the business for future opportunities, funding and growth.

Domains and professional email

What is a domain?
A domain is your website address, such as www.yourbusiness.co.za.
What is a professional email address?
A professional email uses your business domain rather than a free email provider, for example hello@yourbusiness.co.za, admin@yourbusiness.co.za or sales@yourbusiness.co.za.
Why do I need a professional email address?
A domain-based email helps build trust, improve credibility, strengthen your brand and present a more professional image.
How much do professional email accounts cost?
Professional email accounts start from R50 per month per account.
