Privacy Policy

How Ntuthuko Biz Connect handles personal information.

Last updated: June 2026

1. Our commitment

Ntuthuko Biz Connect respects your privacy and processes personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable South African law, including POPIA.

2. Information we collect

We may collect names, contact details, business information, registration and tax information, service requirements and documents voluntarily uploaded through our client intake forms. Clients should never submit passwords, one-time PINs, banking login credentials or information unrelated to the requested service.

3. How we use information

Information is used to communicate with clients, assess service requirements, prepare a scope or quotation, perform agreed work, maintain service records and meet legal obligations. Submitting an intake form does not itself create a service agreement or guarantee an outcome.

4. Storage, sharing and security

We do not sell personal information. Intake records and uploaded documents may be stored using access-controlled cloud infrastructure. Information may be shared with authorised service providers or authorities only where necessary for an agreed service, with appropriate authority, or where required by law. Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used, but no online transmission method is completely risk-free.

5. Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the service, legal, record-keeping or dispute purposes. Clients may ask about retention or request deletion where the law permits it.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information where permitted by law. Contact support@ntuthukobiz.co.za.

7. Cookies and website data

The website may use essential analytics or technical cookies to understand performance and improve the experience. Additional notices will be added when such tools are implemented.
