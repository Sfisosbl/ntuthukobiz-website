Tender Readiness Checklist for South African Businesses

Prepare for South African tenders with a checklist covering CSD, tax, B-BBEE, company records and business capability.

Ntuthuko Biz Connect · Updated 8 June 2026

Tender readiness starts before a bid is advertised. A prepared business can evaluate an opportunity calmly, collect the correct evidence and decide whether it can deliver responsibly.

Get the formal records in order

Common foundations include active CIPC records, correct tax status, CSD registration, banking confirmation and suitable B-BBEE evidence. Every tender has its own conditions.

Prove capability, not only compliance

Buyers may assess experience, references, staffing, equipment, technical methods and financial capacity. A professional company profile should explain what the business can genuinely deliver.

Read every bid carefully

Check compulsory briefings, submission formats, closing times, evaluation criteria and pricing instructions. Missing one mandatory requirement can make a submission non-responsive.

Important: Requirements can change and depend on your circumstances. Confirm current requirements with the relevant authority or a qualified professional.
