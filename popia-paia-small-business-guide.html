POPIA and PAIA Basics for South African Small Businesses

A plain-language introduction to POPIA and PAIA for South African small businesses and responsible information handling.

Ntuthuko Biz Connect · Updated 8 June 2026

Businesses handle personal information every day, from customer telephone numbers to employee records. POPIA and PAIA create responsibilities around how information is used and accessed.

POPIA focuses on responsible processing

Businesses should collect information for a clear purpose, protect it, avoid keeping it unnecessarily and be transparent about how it is used. Practical controls matter.

PAIA supports access to records

PAIA provides a legal process for requesting access to certain records. Organisations may need a manual or supporting information depending on current requirements.

Start with an information map

List what personal information the business collects, why it needs it, where it is stored, who can access it and when it is deleted.

Important: Requirements can change and depend on your circumstances. Confirm current requirements with the relevant authority or a qualified professional.
