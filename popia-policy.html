POPIA Policy

How we approach personal information.

This policy explains our commitment to responsible collection, use, storage and protection of personal information.

Ntuthuko Biz Connect collects personal information only where it is needed to respond to enquiries, deliver services, manage client intake, communicate about programmes, process documents and keep appropriate records.

We aim to process information lawfully, transparently and only for the purpose for which it was collected. Clients should not send passwords, one-time PINs or banking login details.

Requests about personal information can be sent to support@ntuthukobiz.co.za.
