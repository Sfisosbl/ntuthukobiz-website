Practical business education & accountability

Stick To Business

What it is
A structured programme focused on consistency, business fundamentals and better decision-making.
Who it is for
Entrepreneurs who have started but need stronger routines, direction and accountability.
Why it matters
Good ideas lose momentum when pricing, cash flow, customer service and weekly priorities remain unclear.
How it works
Guided learning sessions, practical exercises, peer accountability and implementation checkpoints.
What you gain
Clearer decisions, stronger business habits and a practical plan for consistent progress.

Women-focused empowerment, mentorship & networking

Basadi in Business

What it is
A women-focused business empowerment initiative combining practical learning, mentorship and networking.
Who it is for
Female entrepreneurs at idea, startup or growth stage who want a stronger business foundation and supportive network.
Why it matters
Women-led businesses create jobs and strengthen communities, yet many founders still face gaps in access, confidence, systems and networks.
How it works
An eight-week journey covering validation, registration, compliance, finance, marketing, digital presence and growth readiness.
What you gain
Practical tools, stronger systems, mentorship, peer support and a clearer growth plan.

Opportunity & bid preparation

Tender Masterclasses

What it is
A practical tender readiness programme that explains the documents, systems and decisions behind stronger bids.
Who it is for
Registered businesses and suppliers preparing to approach public or private procurement opportunities.
Why it matters
Businesses often discover missing CSD, tax, B-BBEE, pricing or capability documents only when an opportunity is already closing.
How it works
Focused masterclasses covering compliance files, company profiles, pricing, bid preparation and opportunity assessment.
What you gain
A clearer tender-readiness checklist, better organised records and more confident bid decisions.
