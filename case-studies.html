Case Studies

Scenario-based examples of business bottlenecks and practical interventions.

These examples show the kinds of problems South African entrepreneurs face and how structured support can unlock the next step.

Scenario-Based Case Study

Local Insurance Brokerage in a Busy Shopping Square

Problem: Strong branding and field agents but low sign-ups.

Diagnosis: Weak digital presence, no Google Business Profile, no search visibility, no trust signals.

Ntuthuko Biz intervention: Google Business Profile, lead-generation website, contact forms, search tracking.

Outcome: 24% increase in walk-ins and stronger inbound enquiries.

Business lesson: Offline visibility must be supported by online trust.

Scenario-Based Case Study

Township Funeral Home

Problem: Relied only on word-of-mouth.

Diagnosis: Families increasingly searched online before choosing providers.

Ntuthuko Biz intervention: Website, Google listing, services page, WhatsApp CTA.

Outcome: Improved visibility and more direct enquiries.

Business lesson: Sensitive services still need clear digital information.

Scenario-Based Case Study

Construction Contractor Missing Tender Opportunities

Problem: Repeatedly missed tender opportunities.

Diagnosis: Compliance documents incomplete.

Ntuthuko Biz intervention: Safety file, CSD readiness support, compliance roadmap.

Outcome: Tender-ready administrative profile.

Business lesson: Tender readiness is built before tenders are advertised.

Scenario-Based Case Study

Security Company Growing Too Fast

Problem: Expansion created compliance gaps.

Diagnosis: Operational documentation and PSIRA-related processes were weak.

Ntuthuko Biz intervention: Compliance audit, policy framework, document templates.

Outcome: Improved operational control and reduced compliance risk.

Business lesson: Growth without systems creates hidden risk.
